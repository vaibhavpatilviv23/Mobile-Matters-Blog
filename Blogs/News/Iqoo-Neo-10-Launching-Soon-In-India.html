IQOO is launching its IQOO Neo 10 in India on 26th May

Published on July 1, 2025 by Vaibhav Patil

IQOO is launching its IQOO Neo 10 a new gaming centric smartphone in India on 26th May2025. It comes with Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 along with a custom chip for gaming.

Here are the expected specs for this upcoming smartphone:

  • Design & colors: Its expected to come with similar design to there flagship smartphones. 2 color options will be there Inferno Red & Titanium Chrome.
  • Screen: A 7.78-inch AMOLED screen with upto 144Hz refresh rate targeting gamers with 1.5K resolution.
  • Processor: Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 for great gaming experience & great for heavy users / multitaskers also. It will also come with Q1 Supercomputing chip from IQOO for optimized gaming. It will also come with Vapor Colling Chamber for long gaming hours.
  • RAM & Storage: It will come with 8GB of RAM with 128GB of storage for the base variant. The type will be LPDDR5X for RAM and UFS 4.1 for higher-end storage variants.
  • Battery & charging: It will come with 7,000 mAh battery with fast charging support upto 120W.
  • Software: It will come with Android 15 of out of the box with FunTouch OS 15
  • Camera: It will come with Dual Camera setup at the back with 50MP primary lens with OIS support and 32MP front facing camera.
  • Pricing: Its expected to launch under Rs - 35,000 in India. I will update you with full detailed specs once it launches in India. Launch is scheduled in India for 26th May 2025.
