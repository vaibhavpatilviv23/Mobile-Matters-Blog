Moto Edge 60 Fusion launched in India

July 11, 2025 •by Vaibhav Patil • 5 Min Read

Motorola has launched Moto Edge 60 Fusion successor to the Moto Edge 50 fusion. Moto Edge 60 Fusion comes with Mediatek Dimensity 7400 processor compared to last year Snapdragon 7s Gen 2 Processor.

Design & Colors:

Motorola Edge 60 Fusion also comes with the 3 Pantone color options as of now which includes Pantone Slipstream, Pantone Amazonite and Pantone Zephry. All three colors have vegan leather back which gives premium feel to the device while providing good grip when we hold in the hand. It also has IP 68 Water and dust protection.

Ram & Storage:

In Moto Edge 60 Fusion comes with 2 variants in terms of RAM. One with 8GB of RAM and another with 12GB of RAM both the variants have 256 GB of storage. Both the RAM models have LPDDR4X and storage type is UFS 2.2. It also has Micro SD Card Slot so that if user requires then they can add SD Cards upto 1 TB to expand the storage.

Display:

Moto Edge 60 Fusion comes with 6.67 inch pOLED Quad Curve Display. The resolution of the display is Super HD 1.5K (2712X1220px) @ 446ppi. Refresh rate of the display is upto 120Hz. The display is also Pantone Validates so if you are into Photography work where true colors is important then this can be useful for those users. Display supports HDR 10+ with 10 Bit colors. So watching movies or videos will be a good experience. For protection Moto has provided Corning Gorilla Glass 7i on the front. For authentication it comes with in-display fingerprint scanner.

Battery:

It has 5500mAh battery which is good even for heavy users. Moto is also Providing fast charging support upto 68W with charger provided in the box. 68W fast charger should be able to charge the device fully in less than hour.

Cameras:

Moto Edge 60 Fusion comes with dual Camera’s at the back while third is can 3in1 Sensor to improve image quality. The primary camera is Sony 50MP LYTIA 700C sensor with F/1.8 aperture and it also OIS so it can be helpful while video recording and night shots. Second is Ultrawide angle camera with 13MP F/2.2 which also act as Macro Camera. Third is 3 in 1 Light sensor (Ambient light + Flicker reduction + RGB Sensor) which can help both primary and secondary camera to improve photo and video quality). On the front there is 32 MP camera f/2.2 for selfies. For video recording both front and back cameras are capable for shooting videos upto 4k @ 30 fps.

Speaker & Connectivity:

Moto Edge 60 Fusion has dual stereo speakers with support for Dolby Atomos. For the ports it has USB C port which support fast charging. Sadly, there no headphone jack. It also comes with Bluetooth 5.4 to connect headphones and devices. Device also supports 5G and Wifi 6.

Software:

Moto Edge 60 Fusion comes with clean Android 15 out of the box with some new AI features integrated into OS. Motorola promises upto 3 years of OS updates and 4 years of security patches.

Price:

Moto’s Edge 60 fusion starts in India at Rs 22,999 for base variant that comes with 8GB of RAM and 256 GB of storage. And there is another variant that comes with 12GB of RAM but storage is same that is 256GB that is priced at 24,999. This device is available for sell on Flipkart as well as Motorola India website.
